ingredients:
- 280g all-purpose flour
- 4g ground ginger
- 4g baking soda (previously 5g)
- 4g ground cinnamon (previously 4g)
- 1.5g ground cloves (previously 1g)
- 2g salt
- 165g margarine (or 120g crisco + 15g water)
- 200g white sugar
- 1 egg
- 15ml water
- 60ml (80g) molasses
- 25g white sugar

directions:
- Preheat oven to 350F (175C)
- Sift together flour, ginger, baking soda, cinnamon, cloves and salt, set aside
- Cream together margarine and sugar until light and fluffy
- Beat in one egg then stir in water and molasses
- Gradually stir the sifted ingredients into the molasses mixture
- Shape dough into walnut sized balls and roll them in the remaining sugar
- Place on cookie sheet 2 inches apart and flatten slightly
- Bake for 8-10 minutes or longer until lightly cracked on top
- Allow to cool completely on wire rack
